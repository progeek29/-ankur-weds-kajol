How it all began

Our Story

It started in 2016, five houses apart, close enough that their paths crossed constantly on the same street, passing smiles that meant more than either of them admitted at first. What began as quiet, familiar glances slowly deepened into something neither could ignore.

Then, one day, Ankur sent Kajol a simple Facebook request. Conversations followed, easy and unhurried, building on everything that had already been growing between them in silence. Two years of unspoken affection later, in 2018, Ankur finally found the courage to ask, and she said yes.

Loving each other quietly wasn’t always simple. She was Bengali, he was from Uttar Pradesh, and with their brothers already close friends, they chose to keep their relationship hidden for years, carefully planning stolen moments together despite living just steps apart.

But through every season, Ankur showed up for her, again and again, without hesitation. During the pandemic, when her mother urgently needed an oxygen cylinder amid a nationwide shortage, he didn’t think twice about his own safety or isolation. All that mattered to him was her family, and he made sure they had what they needed, whatever the risk to himself.

Later, when life asked her to move for work, he stepped back from everything he’d built, choosing without a second thought to start fresh beside her instead.

In 2025, they finally told their families the truth they’d carried quietly for years. Hesitation slowly softened into blessings, and by June, they were engaged.

This November, nearly a decade after two neighbors first exchanged smiles on the same street, they’ll finally become husband and wife, proof that some love stories are built not on grand gestures, but on someone who simply never stops showing up.
